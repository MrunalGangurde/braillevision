⠃⠗⠁⠊⠇⠇⠑ how to use BrailleVision
1
add your recognition key
Open ⚙ settings and paste your recognition engine key. It stays in memory only — never saved. Enter your recognition API key. You can get one from your provider's developer console.
2
start camera or upload an image
Use live camera for real-time scanning, or switch to Upload tab to scan a photo from your phone. Both embossed paper and printed Braille work.
3
position Braille in the frame
Hold the Braille flat inside the corner markers. Side lighting from a lamp or window reveals embossed dots best. 15–30 cm distance is ideal.
4
scan — single or auto
Press Scan for one capture, or enable Auto to scan continuously every few seconds. Decoded English appears on the right and is read aloud automatically.
5
review and export
Each result card has speak 🔊, copy ⎘, and enlarge ⤢ buttons. Export all decoded text as a .txt file from the top-right of the results panel.
